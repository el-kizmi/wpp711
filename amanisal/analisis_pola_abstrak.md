# ANALISIS POLA PENULISAN ABSTRAK JURNAL AMANISAL

## 1. RINGKASAN EKSEKUTIF

Dari 10 jurnal yang dianalisis, ditemukan **pola penulisan abstrak yang sangat konsisten** dengan struktur IMRAD (Introduction, Methods, Results, and Discussion) yang diadaptasi untuk konteks perikanan tangkap Indonesia.

---

## 2. STRUKTUR ABSTRAK UMUM

### Pola Umum yang Ditemukan:

```
[BACKGROUND/KONTEKS] → [TUJUAN] → [METODE] → [HASIL] → [KESIMPULAN/IMPLIKASI]
```

### Urutan Wajib:
1. **Kalimat Pembuka (Background)**: 1-2 kalimat tentang konteks/latar belakang masalah
2. **Pernyataan Masalah (Problem Statement)**: Identifikasi kesenjangan penelitian
3. **Tujuan Penelitian (Research Objective)**: Kalimat "This study aims to..."
4. **Metodologi (Methods)**: Pendekatan dan teknik pengumpulan data
5. **Temuan Utama (Key Findings)**: Hasil penelitian dalam angka/kata
6. **Implikasi/Kesimpulan (Implications)**: Signifikansi dan rekomendasi

---

## 3. POLA KALIMAT PER BAGIAN

### A. BACKGROUND/KONTEKS
**Pola yang digunakan:**
- "[Topic] plays a vital role in [context]..."
- "Despite the considerable potential of [topic]..."
- "The [topic] sector requires [something] to support..."
- "Indonesia merupakan negara dengan potensi [topic] yang besar..."

**Contoh dari jurnal:**
- "The Kluwut Fishing Port in Brebes Regency plays a vital role in supporting fishing activities..."
- "The capture fisheries sector requires data-based management to support the sustainability..."

### B. TUJUAN PENELITIAN
**Pola yang digunakan:**
- "This study aims to [verb]..."
- "This research seeks to investigate..."
- "The objectives of this study are to..."
- "Penelitian ini bertujuan untuk menganalisis..."

**Contoh dari jurnal:**
- "This study aims to identify the current condition of port facilities..."
- "This study aims to analyze fishermen's perception..."
- "This study examines the integration of airspace utilization..."

### C. METODOLOGI
**Pola yang digunakan:**
- "The research method employed is [method]..."
- "Data were collected through [techniques]..."
- "The study was conducted at [location] from [time period]..."
- "[Number] respondents were involved as respondents..."

**Contoh dari jurnal:**
- "This research employs a normative juridical approach..."
- "Data collection techniques including observation, interviews, and documentation..."
- "A mixed-methods approach was employed, integrating quantitative surveys from 180 respondents..."

### D. HASIL PENELITIAN
**Pola yang digunakan:**
- "The results indicate/expand/show that..."
- "Findings reveal that..."
- "The results demonstrate..."
- "Hasil penelitian menunjukkan..."

**Contoh dari jurnal:**
- "The results indicate that SST and Chlorophyll-a at the study site fluctuate..."
- "Findings reveal that while primary facilities at PPI Oeba are relatively functional (mean score: 4.03)..."
- "The findings reveal that the average monthly household income of fishers declined sharply..."

### E. KESIMPULAN/IMPLIKASI
**Pola yang digunakan:**
- "These results underscore that..."
- "This study highlights the operational implications..."
- "The study proposes..."
- "These findings indicate that..."

**Contoh dari jurnal:**
- "These results underscore that strengthening adaptive fisheries policies..."
- "This study underscores the imperative of transforming fishing ports..."
- "As a key contribution, the study proposes an integrated air-sea surveillance governance model..."

## 4. KARAKTERISTIK KHUSUS JURNAL AMANISAL

### A. Bahasa
- **Abstrak dalam Bahasa Inggris** (semua 10 jurnal)
- **Kata kunci dalam Bahasa Inggris**
- **Isi artikel dalam Bahasa Indonesia**

### B. Panjang Abstrak
- **Rata-rata**: 150-250 kata
- **Minimum**: ~100 kata (Document 6)
- **Maksimum**: ~300 kata (Document 9)

### C. Informasi Metadata
Setiap abstrak dilengkapi:
- tanggal diterima (Received)
- tanggal diterima untuk dipublikasikan (Accepted)
- tanggal online (Online)
- tipe artikel (Research Article)
- kata kunci (4-6 kata kunci)
- penulis korespondensi

### D. Gaya Penulisan
1. **Objektif**: Menggunakan sudut pandang ketiga
2. **Teknis**: Menggunakan istilah spesifik perikanan
3. **Kuantitatif**: Menyertakan data numerik dalam hasil
4. **Kontekstual**: Merujuk pada regulasi/peraturan Indonesia

## 6. TEMPLATE ABSTRAK UNTUK AI GENERATION

### Template Standar:

```
[BACKGROUND] [PROBLEM STATEMENT] This study aims to [OBJECTIVE]. 
[METHODS DESCRIPTION]. The results [FINDINGS]. 
[IMPLICATIONS/CONCLUSIONS].
```

### Template dengan Detail:

```
The [TOPIC] in [LOCATION] plays a vital role in [CONTEXT], but [PROBLEM]. 
This study aims to [OBJECTIVE] using [METHOD]. 
Data were collected through [TECHNIQUES] involving [NUMBER] respondents, 
and analyzed using [ANALYSIS METHODS]. 
The results indicate that [KEY FINDINGS]. 
[ADDITIONAL FINDINGS]. 
These results underscore that [IMPLICATIONS].
```

---

## 7. REKOMENDASI PROMPT UNTUK AI GENERATION

### Prompt Dasar:
```
Write an abstract for a scientific article about [TOPIC] in Indonesian fisheries context.
Follow this structure:
1. Background (1-2 sentences about the importance of the topic)
2. Problem statement (research gap)
3. Research objective (This study aims to...)
4. Methods (approach, data collection, analysis)
5. Results (key findings with numbers if applicable)
6. Implications (significance and recommendations)

Use formal academic English, 150-250 words.
Include specific data/numbers in the results section.
End with practical implications for fisheries management.
```

### Prompt Lanjutan:
```
Write a scientific abstract about [SPECIFIC TOPIC] in [LOCATION], Indonesia.

Context: [BRIEF BACKGROUND]
Problem: [SPECIFIC PROBLEM]
Methods: [APPROACH] with [DATA COLLECTION] involving [RESPONDENTS/SAMPLES]
Analysis: [STATISTICAL/ANALYTICAL METHODS]

Requirements:
- Follow IMRAD structure
- Use formal academic English
- 150-250 words
- Include specific numbers/statistics
- End with policy/management implications
- Use terms relevant to Indonesian fisheries
```

## 8. CONTOH ABSTRAK BERDASARKAN POLA

### Contoh 1 (Teknologi):
```
The integration of remote sensing technology in fisheries management has become 
increasingly important for sustainable resource utilization. This study aims to 
analyze the spatial distribution of [SPECIFIC FISH] using satellite imagery in 
[LOCATION]. Data were collected from [PERIOD] using [SATELLITE TYPE] imagery 
and processed through digital image processing techniques. The results indicate 
that [FINDINGS WITH NUMBERS]. These findings underscore the importance of 
technology-based approaches in supporting fisheries management and spatial 
planning in Indonesian waters.
```

### Contoh 2 (Sosial-Ekonomi):
```
Small-scale fisheries play a vital role in supporting coastal livelihoods in Indonesia. 
This study aims to examine [SPECIFIC ASPECT] among [TARGET GROUP] in [LOCATION]. 
A survey method was employed with [NUMBER] respondents, and data were analyzed 
using [METHODS]. The findings reveal that [KEY RESULTS]. These results highlight 
the need for [RECOMMENDATIONS] to enhance the sustainability of small-scale 
fisheries and improve the welfare of fishing communities.
```

## 9. KESIMPULAN

Pola penulisan abstrak jurnal AMANISAL menunjukkan konsistensi yang tinggi dalam:
1. **Struktur**: Mengikuti format IMRAD yang jelas
2. **Bahasa**: Formal akademik Inggris untuk abstrak
3. **Konten**: Kombinasi data kuantitatif dan kualitatif
4. **Konteks**: Selalu merujuk pada kondisi perikanan Indonesia
5. **Implikasi**: Selalu berakhir dengan rekomendasi kebijakan/pengelolaan

Pola ini sangat cocok untuk dikembangkan menjadi prompt yang efektif untuk generasi abstrak ilmiah menggunakan AI.
